# User Testing Report

**Course:** ESOF_423_001_202670  
**Project:** Riffline — Guitar Pedal Board  
**Report date:** November 13, 2026

## Peer Review

The peer review went well overall. Feedback on the interface was positive, with classmates describing it as visually appealing. However, many of the people we spoke with were unfamiliar with guitar pedals and their purpose. This limited how much specific feedback they could provide about the product's audio features and typical use cases.

The interactions were brief, and we found them to have limited relevance to evaluating the product for its intended audience. Our main takeaway was that an appealing interface does not necessarily make the purpose of a guitar pedal clear to someone without that background. The review provided a positive first impression of the interface, but limited evidence about how well it supports a guitarist's workflow.

## Independent User Test

The independent user test provided more specific feedback about the sound and interface. The tester identified the following concerns and requests:

| Area | Tester feedback |
| --- | --- |
| Audio noise | Reduce unwanted feedback noise. |
| Overdrive | Increase the “hardness” of the overdrive sound. |
| Individual pedal controls | Provide clearer indicators of whether each pedal is on or off. |
| Color accessibility | Make the interface more accessible to people with color vision deficiencies. |
| Signal visibility | Add a visual signal display, such as a waveform. |

The audio feedback concerned both unwanted noise and the character of the overdrive effect. The interface feedback highlighted the visibility of individual pedal states, color accessibility, and the desire to see a representation of the audio signal.

These points record the tester's feedback and requested changes; they do not establish the technical cause of the noise or a specific implementation for any change. The notes do not include a step-by-step record of setup, navigation, exact questions, or documentation use, so we cannot draw firm conclusions about those parts of the experience.

## Focused Client User Test

We were unable to meet with the client during this reporting period because he was very busy. As a result, we did not conduct the focused client test and have no client behavior observations to report.

This portion of the testing remains incomplete. A future session is needed to observe the client using the software in a typical use case with minimal guidance and to record his actions, questions, and points of confusion.

## Overall Takeaways

The peer review gave us positive feedback on the interface's appearance, while the independent test produced more actionable feedback about audio behavior, pedal state visibility, accessibility, and signal visualization. Validation of the client's typical workflow remains outstanding.
